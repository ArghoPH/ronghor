The 60-30-10 rule

60%
Neutrals: background and cards. Most of the page.
30%
Secondary color and body text. Structure and variety.
10%
Primary and accent. Buttons, links, badges, wherever you want eyes to go.

Think of a room: walls 60%, furniture 30%, cushions and vases 10%.
